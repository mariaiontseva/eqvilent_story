Essence App
Problem Framing

Context: Essence is an AI-powered cycle-syncing productivity app — a female wellbeing app that aligns tasks, fitness, and self-care to the four menstrual phases.

Problem: Onboarding drop-off is too high. Users must input sensitive health data (cycle dates, symptoms, goals) before seeing value. Cold-start problem — predictions are inaccurate until enough data is logged.

Task:

1. Audit current onboarding funnel — identify where users drop off and why (analytics + session recordings).

2. Reduce data-input friction — explore progressive disclosure: show value first, ask for details later.

3. Solve the B2B vs B2C fork — employees invited by company vs individual signup have different trust levels and motivations.

4. Balance wellness vs productivity framing in first 60 seconds.

5. Design and test 2–3 onboarding variants to improve trial-to-paid conversion.
